Overview

This interview evaluates your ability to communicate technical concepts,
demonstrate product capabilities, and navigate complex dynamics with executive
stakeholders. You will lead a 40-minute mock customer meeting. Our goal is to
understand how you prepare for and run a real customer meeting - this is your
opportunity to show us your approach!

Scenario

FinTechCo is a modern financial services company operating digital payment
services and traditional banking divisions. They're evaluating Anthropic's Claude
Code alongside other AI coding assistants- but they don't use any agentic coding
tools today.

The CTO has invited you to present to their executive team. They want to
understand:

   ●​ How Claude Code works at a high level
   ●​ Why FinTechCo would benefit from it
   ●​ How they should evaluate and roll it out
