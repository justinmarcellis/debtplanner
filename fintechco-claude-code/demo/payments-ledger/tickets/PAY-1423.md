# PAY-1423: Statement fees $0.01 low on round-dollar transactions

**Reporter:** Revenue Assurance · **Priority:** P2 · **Component:** ledger/fees

Our monthly reconciliation of merchant statements against contract pricing (Merchant Services Agreement, schedule B: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction, rounded half-up to the cent) found fee lines that are one cent low. It only happens on some amounts, and they are all round-dollar charges, which are common for subscriptions and gift cards.

| Merchant | Transaction | Amount | Statement fee | Contract fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Harbor Coffee Co. | t_88301 | $15.00 | $0.73 | $0.74 |
| Northwind Fitness | t_90122 | $125.00 | $3.92 | $3.93 |
| Northwind Fitness | t_90547 | $145.00 | $4.5 | $4.51 |

Note the last statement line also prints as `$4.5`, which the merchant portal rejects.

One cent is small, but it is systematic under-collection on our highest-volume price points, and statements that don't tie to the contract are an audit finding.

**Acceptance:** fees match the contract for every amount; statement amounts always show two decimals; a regression test covers the amounts above.
