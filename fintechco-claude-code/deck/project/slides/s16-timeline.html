Here is the path to company-wide, with dates you can hold us to. They assume a kickoff on Monday, January eleventh, after the holidays [CLM-665]; the security review can start next week, and an earlier kickoff moves every date earlier. Weeks one and two are readiness: security review closed, policy configured, cloud routing in place, and baselines captured before anyone starts. Weeks three to eight are a six-week pilot with about thirty people from all three teams. Week nine is the decision gate, where you decide go, extend or stop on the evidence; the answer can be no, including choosing a different tool. Weeks ten to seventeen roll out in four waves, 60, then 100, then 145, then all 180 people by Friday, May seventh. Implementation complexity: low for installation, which is one command [CLM-012]; medium for policy and cloud routing; and medium for enablement, because this is your first agentic tool. (75 seconds)
