If asked about cost: Anthropic’s documentation puts the enterprise average at about 150 to 250 dollars per developer per month, or about 13 dollars per active day, with 90 percent of users under 30 dollars a day [CLM-619]. Controls: spend limits at organization, group or member level [CLM-685]; on Bedrock or Google Cloud it bills to your cloud account under your budgets [CLM-620]; and simple habits like using Sonnet for most work and clearing context between tasks [CLM-696]. Your actual price would be quoted by the account team.
