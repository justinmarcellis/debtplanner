This is the picture your architects will ask for. Engineers run Claude Code on their laptops, and the same agent runs headless in CI. A couple of environment variables route every model request to Amazon Bedrock in a FinTechCo AWS account, or to Google Cloud’s Agent Platform, formerly Vertex AI, in a FinTechCo GCP project [CLM-686] [CLM-687]; the documentation recommends a dedicated account or project, and pinned model versions [CLM-688]. Your existing commercial agreement applies to usage through those clouds [CLM-614]. Your cloud’s identity and access management decides who may call the model, and spend shows up in your own cloud billing under your budgets [CLM-620]. On Bedrock, AWS states that model providers, Anthropic included, have no access to your prompts or logs; check the retention setting for the specific model [CLM-615]. Around it sit three things you control: policy, audit, and identity and cost. Controls make it safe to try; the next question is how you will know it worked. (75 seconds; skip at the 0:30 checkpoint)
