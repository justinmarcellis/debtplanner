Measuring business impact matters to you both, so I start with an honest note: the evidence on AI coding productivity is mixed. Field experiments with almost five thousand developers found 26 percent more completed tasks [CLM-623]; a 2025 trial found experienced open-source developers took 19 percent longer [CLM-650]. So we measure on your work, against your own baseline, the same way for every tool. Five tiers. Adoption: who uses it and how often, visible in weeks from Claude Code’s own telemetry [CLM-656]. Flow: hours saved and pull-request cycle time, then lead time to production, using the DORA definitions [CLM-655]. Quality is the guardrail tier: change failure rate and escaped defects must not get worse; DORA’s 2024 research linked AI adoption with lower delivery stability, so this is a hard gate [CLM-653]. Experience: a short survey, including whether juniors are still learning. Business outcomes show up over months: roadmap throughput and time to launch. Every number has a baseline captured before anyone starts. (95 seconds)
