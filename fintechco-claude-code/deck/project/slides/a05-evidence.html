If asked for the evidence: it is mixed, and I would rather show you all of it. Field experiments at Microsoft, Accenture and a Fortune 100 company with 4,867 developers found 26 percent more completed tasks, with bigger gains for less experienced developers [CLM-623]. A Google trial with 96 engineers, published as a preprint, estimated about 21 percent less time on task with a wide interval [CLM-652]. METR’s 2025 trial found 16 experienced open-source developers took 19 percent longer, while believing they were faster [CLM-650]; their 2026 update estimated speedups but called the signal unreliable [CLM-651]. DORA 2024 found productivity and flow up but delivery stability down [CLM-653]. And Anthropic’s own 2026 trial found juniors who delegated learned less than those who asked conceptual questions [CLM-626]. That is why the pilot measures, with quality guardrails.
