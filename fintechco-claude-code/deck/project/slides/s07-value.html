Why would FinTechCo benefit? Three reasons, in your order of priority. Team productivity: learning an unfamiliar service, writing tests and chasing defects become tasks the agent carries while engineers review. Field experiments with almost five thousand developers found 26 percent more completed tasks with an AI coding assistant [CLM-623]; one trial with experienced open-source developers found the opposite [CLM-650], which is exactly why we measure on your work. Shipping new products: hours saved go back into roadmap work; in our base case that is about eight full-time engineers of capacity, and a data scientist can turn an analysis into a client-ready dashboard without a front-end developer; you will see that in Act two. Safely: one policy, one audit trail and your own cloud accounts cover every team [CLM-614]. CTO, proof of enterprise adoption matters to you: Anthropic’s financial-services page carries statements from named firms, including one attributed to Citi; I will send you the page so you can read the exact wording [CLM-631]. Treat that as a reason to run a pilot, not as a forecast for FinTechCo. (75 seconds)
