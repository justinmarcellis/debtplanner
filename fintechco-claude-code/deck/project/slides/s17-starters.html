Two starters per team from the use-case catalog · protocol in the evaluation plan

These are the starter use cases, two per team, each chosen for a measurable baseline, a small blast radius, meaning little can go wrong if it fails, and a visible result in the first week. Software engineers: onboarding to an unfamiliar service, and a test-first defect fix, exactly what you saw in Act one. Data scientists: data acquisition and exploration, and turning a notebook into a tested package. SREs: read-only incident triage, and turning a manual runbook into a tested script. Each has success criteria and a guardrail in the evaluation plan. Head of Digital Transformation, treat this as a starting list for your team leads to adjust to their own backlogs.
