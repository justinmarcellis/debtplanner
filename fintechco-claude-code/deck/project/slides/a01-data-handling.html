Sources: [CLM-615] [CLM-614] [CLM-602] [CLM-603] [CLM-605] [CLM-600] [CLM-604] [CLM-601]

If asked where data goes: on Bedrock, calls use your AWS credentials and IAM and bill to your account, and AWS says model providers including Anthropic cannot see your prompts or logs; retention is set per Region, and a few newer models need AWS-side retention for review, so check the specific model [CLM-615] [CLM-614]. On Google Cloud I have not verified the retention terms for Claude, so your GCP team should confirm them in your Google contract. Direct with Anthropic, standard retention is thirty days; qualified Enterprise organizations can turn on zero data retention, which also switches off features that store data, such as cloud sessions [CLM-602] [CLM-603]. On the laptop, transcripts stay locally for thirty days by default; telemetry is off by default through Bedrock or Google Cloud, and one setting switches it off on the direct route [CLM-602] [CLM-605]. Under the commercial terms Anthropic does not train on your content unless you choose to share it [CLM-600]; traffic is TLS 1.2 or higher [CLM-604]; you own the outputs, and there is an IP defense commitment whose exclusions your counsel should read [CLM-601].
