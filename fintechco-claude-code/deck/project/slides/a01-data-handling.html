Sources: [CLM-615] [CLM-614] [CLM-602] [CLM-603] [CLM-605] [CLM-600] [CLM-604] [CLM-601]

If asked where data goes: on Bedrock, inference runs in your AWS account, and AWS says model providers including Anthropic cannot see your prompts or logs; retention is set per Region, and a few newer models need AWS-side retention for review, so check the specific model [CLM-615] [CLM-614]. On Google Cloud I have not verified the retention terms for Claude, so your GCP team should confirm them in your Google contract. Direct with Anthropic, standard retention is thirty days, with zero data retention available on Enterprise [CLM-602] [CLM-603]. On the laptop, transcripts stay locally for thirty days by default and telemetry is off unless you turn it on [CLM-602] [CLM-605]. Under the commercial terms Anthropic does not train on your content [CLM-600]; traffic is TLS 1.2 or higher [CLM-604]; you own the outputs, and there is an IP defense commitment whose exclusions your counsel should read [CLM-601].
