Here is how it fits each of your three teams, in their own stacks. Your one hundred and twenty software engineers build payments, mobile apps and banking infrastructure in Python, TypeScript and Java. Their first wins are onboarding to unfamiliar services and test-first defect fixes, where the failing test is written before the fix; then cross-stack features and framework upgrades (UC-SWE-01 to 05). Your forty data scientists build fraud, credit-scoring and customer-behavior models in Python, Jupyter, pandas, scikit-learn and TensorFlow. Their wins are data acquisition and cleaning, turning notebooks into tested production code, and client-ready dashboards (UC-DS-01 to 05). Your twenty SREs run incidents in Python, shell and Go. Their wins are incident triage from logs and metrics, toil automation and postmortem drafts (UC-SRE-01 to 05). One agent and one policy for all one hundred and eighty people, which is what a single company-wide tool requires. Each team gets one act in the demo, which is next.
