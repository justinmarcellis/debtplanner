If asked what is behind the ROI: these six assumptions, each with three values. Loaded cost is an assumption to replace with your HR figure. Hours saved is bounded by the evidence: the base four hours is ten percent of a week, below the field-experiment results [CLM-623], and the conservative two hours allows for the trial where experienced developers were slower [CLM-650]. Usage cost uses Anthropic’s published enterprise range [CLM-619]. The pilot replaces every assumption with a measured number, and the calculator lets your finance team change any of them.
